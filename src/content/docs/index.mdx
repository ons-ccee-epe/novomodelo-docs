Novomodelo is an open-source solver for multi-stage stochastic hydrothermal dispatch:
it trains an operating policy with Stochastic Dual Dynamic Programming (SDDP) and
simulates it, from the command line or from Python. This site documents the
method and the software together, from the mathematics and worked examples to
installing Novomodelo, running studies and reading their results.

## How the model decides

<div class="home-steps">
  <div class="home-step"><span class="home-step__n">1</span><strong>Imagine the future</strong><p>It generates many inflow scenarios from the streamflow history.</p></div>
  <div class="home-step"><span class="home-step__n">2</span><strong>Dispatch stage by stage</strong><p>At each stage it chooses how much each plant generates to meet the load at the lowest cost.</p></div>
  <div class="home-step"><span class="home-step__n">3</span><strong>Weigh today against tomorrow</strong><p>The future cost function says what it is worth to keep water in the reservoir: that is the value of water.</p></div>
