Novomodelo is an open-source solver for multi-stage stochastic hydrothermal dispatch:
it trains an operating policy with Stochastic Dual Dynamic Programming (SDDP) and
simulates it, from the command line or from Python. This site documents the
method and the software together, from the mathematics and worked examples to
installing Novomodelo, running studies and reading their results.
