## What you provide

- The bus from which the station draws power.
- The source hydro plant, whose reservoir loses the water, and the destination hydro plant, whose reservoir receives it.
- The power drawn per unit of pumped flow, in MW/(m³/s).
- The minimum and maximum pumped flow, in m³/s. The maximum is the installed pump capacity.
- Entry and exit stages, when the station does not exist throughout the study.
- Optionally, flow limits that vary by stage, which replace those of the registry.

## Pumping and the economics

- **No objective cost.** A pumping station has no cost term in the objective function. The cost of pumping is captured implicitly through the energy it consumes: the marginal cost of energy at the connected bus sets the effective cost.
- **Economic incentive.** Pumping is attractive when energy prices are low, for example with excess hydro or renewable generation, and unattractive when prices are high, for example with thermal dispatch at the margin.
- **In the results.** The station's cost column in the simulation output is always zero: its power enters the bus balance.
