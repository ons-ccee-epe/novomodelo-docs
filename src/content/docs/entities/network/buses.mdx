## What you provide

- A bus identifier and name.
- The `bus_id` of every entity that attaches to it: hydro plants, thermal plants, non-controllable sources, pumping stations, energy contracts and the two ends of each line.
- Optionally, deficit segments for this bus, which replace the case default. They cannot vary by stage.
- The load at the bus, supplied as scenario data (see the [Load](/entities/network/load/) page).

## Subsystems, copper plate and multi-bus

- **Subsystem.** In the Brazilian grid, a subsystem is a region of the interconnected system (SE/CO, S, NE, N). Real-world dispatch studies often model buses at that scale, but finer decompositions are equally valid.
- **Single bus (copper plate).** All generation and load sit in one node: no flow limits, no transmission losses and no locational price differentiation. It is the right starting point to isolate dispatch economics from network effects.
- **Multi-bus.** Buses are connected by lines. Once a line capacity binds, each bus resolves its own marginal price, and dispatch in one bus cannot freely substitute for a shortfall in another.
