## What you provide

- The deficit segments, in order: for each one, a depth in MW and a cost per MWh. The costs must be positive and strictly increasing.
- The last segment has no depth, which makes it unbounded.
- A case-level default for all buses, optionally replaced for a given bus.
- Deficit segments cannot vary by stage.

## How the deficit cost is resolved

Deficit segments come from exactly two sources: the segments declared on the bus in its registry, and, when the bus declares none, the default of the case-level penalty file. The first MW of unmet demand cost the first segment cost, the next ones cost the second segment cost, and so on.

## The mirror: excess

Excess generation is the opposite slack of the same balance: a small regularization cost removes spurious surplus generation. Unlike deficit, its cost has no per-bus field, only the case default, which may be refined by stage.

## In the results

Each bus and block reports its deficit in MW and its deficit energy in MWh. The stage cost breakdown has a column with the cost of unserved load.
